Plot Twist Daily makes short, narrated story videos: everyday payback stories, workplace drama and twists you don't see coming, told over calm animated backgrounds.

We publish them on our own pages on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Our desktop app, StoryReels, writes the voiceover, adds captions and backgrounds, and posts the finished video to our own accounts.

Our TikTok integration

StoryReels uses TikTok Login Kit so the owner of the Plot Twist Daily TikTok account can connect it, and the Content Posting API to publish our own videos to that account. It only ever posts to the account that connected it.
