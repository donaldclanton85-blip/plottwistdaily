This policy explains what information the Plot Twist Daily website and the StoryReels app ("the app") handle.

1. The website

This website has no sign-up, no forms, no ads and no tracking cookies of our own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

2. The app and TikTok

When the Plot Twist Daily TikTok account is connected through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives from TikTok:

  • basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), used to show which account is connected;
  • an access token and refresh token, used only to upload and publish our own videos to that account.

This information is stored only on the computer that runs the app. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. We do not collect information about TikTok viewers or other users.

3. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. Disconnecting the app in TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) revokes access; the stored tokens then stop working and are deleted by the app.

4. Children

The app and website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
